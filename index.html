Say this

Your head · almost all judgment

It knows your field. It doesn’t know you.

Swap in your own task

Let it interview you.

Say this

I want to build a skill for how I review a proposal from a vendor. Interview me to learn how I think about it. Ask one question at a time, and let each answer shape your next question.

Focus on my judgment: what I look at first, what good looks like to me, and what a smart new hire would most likely get wrong.

Stop at 15 questions, or sooner if you have what you need. Then build the skill from what you learned.
